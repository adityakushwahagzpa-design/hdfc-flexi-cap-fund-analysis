# HDFC Flexi Cap Fund – Returns, Risk & Portfolio Analysis

A research report and Excel model on **HDFC Flexi Cap Fund (Direct – Growth)**, with data up to 30 October 2026.

## Data
10 years of daily AMFI NAV data for the fund, the NIFTY 500 and the BSE Sensex, plus 37 flexi cap peer funds.

## Analysis
- Lumpsum CAGR for 1–10 years
- Monthly SIP and 10% step-up SIP returns using **XIRR**
- Risk measures from 36 monthly returns: standard deviation, **Sharpe, Sortino, beta, alpha**
- Portfolio review: valuation ratios, sector weights vs category, changes in top holdings

## Key findings
- **15.5%** 5-year CAGR vs 8.5% for the NIFTY 500
- Highest 3-year Sharpe ratio (**0.78**) of the 37 flexi cap funds, with lower volatility than the index
- A ₹10,000 monthly SIP: ₹12 lakh invested grew to **₹28.4 lakh** over 10 years (16.4% XIRR)

## Tools
Excel (XIRR, SUMPRODUCT, SLOPE, INDEX/MATCH, live charts) · 3-page factsheet-style PDF
